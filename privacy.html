Privacy

GCM Track · last updated November 22, 2026

No account, no tracking

GCM Track doesn't ask for your name, email or location, has no ads, and uses no analytics or tracking.

What stays on your phone

Your chosen station, saved trains and the tracks your phone has seen are stored only on your device. Deleting the app removes them.

Track alerts

If you turn on an alert, your phone's push address (created by Apple for this app) and the train numbers you chose are stored on our alert server so it can notify you. Nothing else is stored with them. Turning an alert off deletes that alert; one-day alerts are deleted automatically after the day passes, and everything is deleted if Apple reports the push address no longer works.

Train data

Departure times and tracks come from the MTA's public data feeds. The app asks those servers for the current departures; it sends them nothing about you.
